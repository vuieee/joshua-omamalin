EVERYDAY GEAR: WHAT I USE AND WHAT I WANT

Published 2026-11-06 · 3 min read

Some things earn a place in my everyday routine; others stay on my wishlist because they solve a small problem in a thoughtful way. Here are two pieces of gear I use and four I would like to try.

In My Daily Rotation

CMF Buds 2a

I use the CMF Buds 2a regularly, and so far they have been treating me well. They have a good amount of bass and a very respectable battery life. The quality is also very good despite it being more of a budget option, then again CMF (A branch of Nothing) are known for their value.

Logitech MX Vertical

The MX Vertical is the other daily driver. Its upright, handshake-like shape is a welcome change from a conventional mouse, and I like how naturally it fits into my right hand (I am right-handed) as it is quite large so the normal mouse would be uncomfortable. The battery on this thing is impressive, I haven't charged this thing in a month and it's still going, which is really what I want from a mouse I use every day.

On My Wishlist

Flow Satchel (4L)

A compact four-liter satchel seems like a nice middle ground: enough room for daily essentials without the bulk of a backpack. I would like to have a small grab-and-go bag for the days when I only need to carry a few things.

Telescopic Fisher Space Pen

The telescoping design is what gets me: it stays compact in a pocket, then extends into a more comfortable full-size pen when it is time to write. Add Fisher's pressurized refill, designed to write in demanding conditions, and it feels like a clever bit of practical engineering rather than just another pen.

MOONDROP CHU III

I am curious about trying a wired in-ear monitor from MOONDROP. The CHU III would give me a simple listening option that does not need charging or wireless pairing. I would like to see how its fit and sound compare with the wireless earbuds I already use.

Parker Jotter XL

The Jotter XL takes the familiar click-pen idea and gives it a larger profile. I like the clean, classic look, and the extra size sounds appealing for longer writing sessions. It seems like the sort of everyday pen that can feel considered without being fussy.

That is the split for now: two things I already rely on, and four I would be happy to add to the rotation. The best gear is not always the most complicated; sometimes it is just the thing that makes an ordinary day a little easier.
